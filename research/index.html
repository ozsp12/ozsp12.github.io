Research

Research interests

My research spans theoretical physics, quantitative modeling and computational methods.

Warp Drive Theory

I investigate general-relativistic warp-drive geometries, their matter sources, field equations and physical constraints.

Black Holes and Wormholes

I study stability, thermodynamics and mathematical properties of compact and nontrivial spacetime geometries.

Econophysics

I apply methods from statistical physics and complex systems to markets, wealth distributions and economic dynamics.

Artificial Intelligence

I design machine-learning and analytics systems, with emphasis on scalable modeling, anomaly detection and applied AI.

Quantum Computing

I explore quantum information, quantum algorithms and hybrid quantum-classical computational methods.

Mathematical Physics and Education

I develop rigorous expository work connecting geometry, mechanics, mathematical problem solving and computational visualization.
