About

I was born in Belém, Pará, Brazil, and completed part of my secondary education in Greenwood, Indiana, United States. I earned my BSc and MSc in Physics from the University of Campinas and my PhD in Physics from the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro.

I am a researcher at the Graduate Program in Multidisciplinary Applied Physics at UFRJ. My interests include general relativity, warp-drive spacetimes, classical and quantum gravity, cosmology, quantum computing, computational physics, econophysics, artificial intelligence, and pure and applied mathematics.

Academic background

  • PhD in Physics — Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ)
  • MSc in Physics — University of Campinas (UNICAMP)
  • BSc in Physics — University of Campinas (UNICAMP)
  • MBA in Big Data & Analytics — University of São Paulo (USP)
  • Postgraduate Specialization in Quantum Computing — SENAI-CIMATEC

Professional experience

Approximately 15 years of private-sector experience in data science, analytics, artificial intelligence, technical leadership, and consulting across the financial, healthcare, technology, education, and industrial sectors.

I work as an artificial intelligence consultant and data science specialist, with expertise in machine learning, neural networks, transformers, natural language processing, computer vision, predictive modeling, and large language models.

I have contributed to more than one hundred projects across streaming, healthcare, insurance, banking, e-commerce, retail, naval defense, education, and technology, applying AI to real industrial and business problems.
