Research

My research spans theoretical physics, computation, mathematics, complex systems, and data-driven methods.

Gravitation and Cosmology

I study general relativity, warp drive spacetimes, exact solutions of Einstein’s equations, differential geometry, geodesic motion, black holes, wormholes, energy-momentum tensors, 3+1 formulations, junction conditions, causal structure, and relativistic cosmology, including observational relations along the past light cone.

Complex Systems and Computational Physics

My work includes econophysics, nonlinear dynamics, network science, stochastic processes, scaling relations, agent-based models, numerical analysis, ordinary and partial differential equations, symbolic computation, dynamical simulations, and scientific machine learning.

Quantum Computing, Artificial Intelligence and Data Science

I investigate quantum information, quantum algorithms and simulation, quantum machine learning, large language models, natural-language processing, recommendation systems, time-series modelling, model evaluation, retrieval-augmented and agentic systems, statistical modelling, forecasting, optimisation, experimentation, and decision-oriented analytics.

Applied and Pure Mathematics

My mathematical interests include optimisation, differential equations, Lie symmetries, numerical analysis, operations research, inequalities and extremal problems, real-rooted polynomials, analytic number theory, algebra, mathematical analysis, and geometry.

Broader Interdisciplinary Interests

I also work at the interface of education, scientific computing, social physics, and philosophy of science, with particular interest in reproducible computational methods, quantitative descriptions of collective behaviour, and the relation between mathematical structure, physical interpretation, observation, and scientific models.
