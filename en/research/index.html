Research

My research is organized into primary lines, applied and computational work, and broader interdisciplinary interests. Each line is described by the problem being studied and the methods used to address it.

Primary research

Fundamental and theoretical problems

Warp-Drive Spacetimes

Problem. Determine which superluminal geometries can satisfy Einstein’s equations and what matter content they require.

Methods. Exact solutions, 3+1 decompositions, fluid descriptions, junction conditions, symbolic computation.

General Relativity and Gravity

Problem. Understand the geometry, causal structure and material interpretation of relativistic spacetimes.

Methods. Differential geometry, Einstein field equations, exact solutions and fields in curved spacetime.

Cosmology

Problem. Test relativistic cosmological models against observations without discarding light-cone effects.

Methods. Null geodesics, classical cosmological tests, statistical comparison and large-scale structure models.

Econophysics and Complex Systems

Problem. Explain distributions, inequality, cycles and collective behaviour in economic systems.

Methods. Statistical distributions, time-series analysis, nonlinear dynamics and interacting-agent models.

Pure Mathematics

Problem. Study inequalities, algebraic structure, number-theoretic questions and geometric invariance.

Methods. Mathematical analysis, algebra, analytic number theory, geometry and rigorous proof.

Applied / computational research

Models, algorithms and computation

Computational Physics

Problem. Solve physical models that are analytically intractable or require numerical experiments.

Methods. Numerical integration, differential equations, symbolic computation, simulation and scientific machine learning.

Quantum Computing

Problem. Identify quantum computational approaches useful for physics, optimisation and learning.

Methods. Quantum circuits, information theory, simulation, algorithms and quantum machine learning.

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

Problem. Learn useful representations and predictions from complex structured and unstructured data.

Methods. Neural networks, transformers, representation learning, supervised learning and generative models.

Data Science and Advanced Analytics

Problem. Turn observational and operational data into reliable inference, forecasts and decisions.

Methods. Statistical modelling, causal inference, time series, experimentation, optimisation and visual analytics.

Applied Mathematics

Problem. Formulate and solve mathematical models arising in science, industry and decision problems.

Methods. ODEs and PDEs, numerical analysis, operations research, optimisation and symmetry methods.

Broader interests

Education, society and foundations

Education and Scientific Computing

Problem. Make mathematical and computational reasoning more transparent in physics education.

Methods. Computational examples, simulations, visual explanations and reproducible notebooks.

Social Physics

Problem. Describe collective human behaviour with quantitative models.

Methods. Networks, population models, statistical physics and dynamical systems.

Philosophy of Science

Problem. Clarify how mathematical structures, observation and physical interpretation are related.

Methods. Conceptual analysis informed by the foundations of physics and mathematics.
