I welcome messages concerning research collaborations, invited talks, scientific publications, teaching, artificial intelligence, data science, and applied research projects. For commercial or consulting enquiries, please include a concise description of the problem, expected scope, and proposed timeline.

Requests concerning graduate admission or formal academic supervision should follow the official procedures of the Graduate Program in Multidisciplinary Applied Physics at UFRJ. I may discuss potential research topics, but admission decisions and institutional arrangements are not made by personal email.

I cannot provide detailed reviews of unsolicited manuscripts, extensive calculations, or privately developed theories. This is particularly true for messages claiming to have disproved general relativity, quantum mechanics, or modern mathematics in three pages. Genuine and well-defined questions about established physics, mathematics, or computational methods are more likely to receive an answer.

I receive a substantial volume of correspondence, so replies may take some time. Clear questions usually travel faster than manifestos.
