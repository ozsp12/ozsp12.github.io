About

I was born in Belém, Pará, Brazil, and completed part of my secondary education in Greenwood, Indiana, United States. I earned my BSc and MSc in Physics at the University of Campinas (UNICAMP) and my PhD in Physics at the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ). I am currently a researcher affiliated with the Instituto de Física da Universidade Federal do Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ). My trajectory connects theoretical physics, mathematics and scientific computing with data science and artificial intelligence, combining academic research with long-term experience in applied quantitative work.

Industry, Data & AI

Over approximately 15 years in the private sector, I have worked in data science, artificial intelligence, advanced analytics, quantitative modelling, consulting, and technical leadership across finance, healthcare, technology, education, media, and industry. My trajectory includes roles as Data Scientist, Lead Data Scientist, Head of Data Science, Tech Lead, Chapter Leader, and AI specialist, leading teams and projects from problem formulation and model development to deployment, monitoring, governance, and communication with technical and executive stakeholders.

Teaching & Education

I am an invited professor at Faculdade Sírio-Libanês, teaching artificial intelligence, data science and advanced analytics, and at Faculdade Israelita de Ciências da Saúde Albert Einstein, teaching biostatistics and quantitative methods in health. My earlier teaching experience includes undergraduate Calculus, as well as technical-scientific writing, co-authorship, and review of educational materials in physics.

International experience

I completed part of my secondary education in Greenwood, Indiana, United States, and have C2-level English proficiency. My academic and technical work includes scientific writing in English, collaboration with international research teams, and participation in ARDD24 in Copenhagen, Denmark.
